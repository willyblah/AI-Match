# VN Portrait Style And Acting

Use this reference when a visual novel portrait request names a genre, period, or mood and the final prompt needs concrete style, palette, expression, and small upper-body acting.

## General Portrait Defaults

- Framing: head to chest or head to waist; no legs or feet.
- Canvas: vertical 3:4.
- Acting: subtle upper-body expression, not animation or action.
- Rendering: readable face, clear silhouette, clean linework, controlled shading.
- Background: flat chroma-key only, normally `#FF00FF`.

## American Campus Romance

Style:
- contemporary romance VN illustration
- clean anime-inspired or webtoon-adjacent linework
- natural young-adult proportions
- casual American campus clothing: hoodie, varsity jacket, denim jacket, flannel, campus sweater, tote bag, backpack strap

Palette:
- warm autumn campus colors, denim blue, cream, burgundy, navy, heather gray
- avoid making clothing magenta/purple dominant unless requested

Expressions and acting:
- warm confident smile
- shy but curious glance
- relaxed shoulders
- one hand on backpack strap
- notebook held to chest
- casual hand in hoodie pocket

Prompt keywords:
- `American college romance visual novel`
- `contemporary campus fashion`
- `warm sincere expression`
- `soft overcast afternoon lighting rendered on the character only`

## Youthful / Coming-Of-Age / Melancholy Romance

Style:
- lower saturation
- soft linework or sketch-like line emphasis
- subdued shadows
- delicate facial expression

Palette:
- muted blues, gray, off-white, soft brown, desaturated red accents

Expressions and acting:
- tired eyes
- restrained smile
- looking slightly away
- shoulders drawn in
- hand near collar or sleeve cuff
- notebook, school bag, scarf, or phone held close

Prompt keywords:
- `subdued coming-of-age visual novel portrait`
- `gentle melancholy`
- `low saturation`
- `simple clean linework`

## Japanese School Romance

Style:
- Japanese visual novel / light novel character art
- clean crisp linework
- neat school-uniform details
- expressive eyes

Palette:
- navy, white, gray, soft school-uniform accents

Expressions and acting:
- shy smile
- slightly embarrassed glance
- hand near chest
- one hand holding school bag strap

Prompt keywords:
- `Japanese school romance VN portrait`
- `clean light novel character art`
- `school uniform`

## Republic-Era / Historical Chinese Mystery

Style:
- restrained historical VN illustration
- period clothing and accessories
- cooler noir lighting rendered on character only
- elegant but tense posture

Palette:
- ink black, muted teal, ivory, wine red, smoke gray, aged gold

Expressions and acting:
- guarded gaze
- composed grief
- restrained suspicion
- notebook or folded letter held close
- one hand clutching coat lapel or purse

Prompt keywords:
- `Republic-era Chinese mystery visual novel portrait`
- `1930s Shanghai period clothing`
- `restrained noir mood`
- `elegant historical character design`

## Detective / Suspense

Style:
- sharper linework
- stronger under-eye or cheek shadows
- grounded costume details

Palette:
- desaturated colors, dark coat, off-white shirt, muted accent

Expressions and acting:
- narrowed eyes
- guarded half-smile
- clenched jaw
- arms folded
- note card or file held close

Prompt keywords:
- `suspense VN portrait`
- `reserved investigative expression`
- `subtle tension`

## Fantasy / Historical Fantasy

Style:
- decorative costume details
- clean readable silhouette
- restrained ornament so the face remains primary

Palette:
- choose based on role; avoid magenta-dominant designs if using magenta key

Expressions and acting:
- solemn gaze
- ritual composure
- hand near pendant or cloak clasp
- confident calm posture

Prompt keywords:
- `fantasy visual novel portrait`
- `ornate but readable costume`
- `clean character silhouette`

## Comedy / Slice Of Life

Style:
- brighter colors
- simpler shapes
- lively expression

Expressions and acting:
- open smile
- curious tilt
- playful raised eyebrow
- relaxed hand gesture near face

Prompt keywords:
- `slice-of-life VN portrait`
- `bright friendly expression`
- `simple clean character art`
